## About
Sri Advaita Educational Trust is a values-driven State Board educational institution located in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, near the Karnataka–Tamil Nadu border. Founded on the principles of Advaita philosophy, the school blends traditional Indian wisdom with modern pedagogical methods to nurture holistic child development.

## Location
**Address:** Plot No: 13, Manickavasagar Street, Mookandapalli, MGR Nagar, Hosur – 635126, Tamil Nadu, India
**Region:** Near Bangalore border (Karnataka / Tamil Nadu)
**Nearby Landmarks:** Hosur Bus Stand, MGR Nagar

## Programs Offered
- **Nursery (Pre-KG) [In Academic Collaboration with Little Elly]:** Ages 2.5–3.5 | Little Elly H.A.P.P.Y. Curriculum, tactile Montessori play, phonics & sensory exploration
- **Junior KG (LKG) [In Academic Collaboration with Little Elly]:** Ages 3.5–4.5 | Little Elly H.A.P.P.Y. Curriculum, spoken language, pre-math, spatial reasoning
- **Senior KG (UKG) [In Academic Collaboration with Little Elly]:** Ages 4.5–5.5 | Little Elly H.A.P.P.Y. Curriculum, independent reading readiness, school readiness
- **Primary School (Grades 1–5):** Ages 5.5–10 | State Board academic curriculum with foundational inquiry
- **Middle School (Grades 6–8):** Ages 10–13 | State Board STEAM robotics, coding & future-ready skills
